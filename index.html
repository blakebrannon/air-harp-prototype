Air Harp

A prototype for AskWolfie

Camera off No hands Sound locked 0 / 12 strings

With a camera: pinch thumb and index on both hands, pull apart to draw a glowing string, then release to set it. Sweep a fingertip across a string to pluck it. Length sets the pitch — longer is lower, shorter is higher, always on a pentatonic scale. Without a camera: click or tap and drag to draw a string; move across a string to pluck.
